# Observation sheet

One sheet per version per participant, so two per participant. The facilitator fills it in during and straight after the task.

This sheet records **observed behaviour only**: what the participant does and says while working, including think-aloud remarks. Do not record answers to the questionnaire here. Ratings, knowledge-check answers and opinions given in answer to questions go in the participant response template. Keep the two apart.
