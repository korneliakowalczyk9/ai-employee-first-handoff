## About this study

We are looking at how people understand and feel about handing work to an AI employee. You will use a mock-up of an AI employee on a computer and then answer questions about your experience. Nothing in the mock-up is real, and nothing you do in it affects anything or anyone.

This study is run by an individual researcher as a personal portfolio project. It is not run for an employer or a company. To contact the researcher, write to [CONTACT ADDRESS].

- Answer a few background questions.
- Use [one prototype / two versions of a prototype] and answer questions after [it / each one].
- This takes about [25 / 40] minutes.

## What we will collect

- Your answers to the questions.
- A participant code that links your answers together.
- If you choose to record: a screen recording of the prototype, as described below.

We do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details in the questionnaire. You are identified only by your participant code. The researcher knows which code went to which person, because they sent you the invitation, and keeps that list apart from your answers.

## Optional screen recording

Recording your screen is optional. You can take part in exactly the same way without it. Choosing not to record has no effect on whether your answers are used.

**What it is for.** A recording shows what you clicked on the screen and in what order. It helps us understand how you moved through the prototype. It cannot show what you were thinking or noticing, so we also ask you questions. We do not treat a recording as a complete picture of your experience.

**What to record.** Please record only the browser window with the prototype. Before you start, close other windows and turn off notifications so that nothing private appears. Please do not record your microphone or your camera. We do not need your voice or your face.

**How to record.** Use a screen recorder you already have. [DECISION: which recording tools you will recommend, if any, and the steps for each. Check the steps on real devices before sending.]

**How to send it.** You send the recording through a private upload link that comes with your invitation. Name the file with your participant code and Version 1 or Version 2, for example P3-Version-1. Please do not use your name. Recordings are kept apart from your questionnaire answers.

**If you cannot or do not want to record.** Tell us in the questionnaire when you are asked. You will complete the same task and the same questions.

## How your information will be used

The results will be used for a portfolio case study and a write-up of what we found. Short quotes from your written answers may be included. They will not include your name or your participant code. Recordings are never shown or shared.

## Where your information is kept

Your questionnaire answers are stored in the online form tool, Tally. Recordings are stored in a private online folder that only the researcher can open. Recordings are deleted once the researcher has analysed them, and no later than 90 days after the study ends. Anonymised answers and notes may be kept after that.

To ask for your answers or recording to be deleted, email [CONTACT ADDRESS] with your participant code. They will be deleted and you will get a confirmation. You can ask at any time until the results have been written up. After that, your answers may no longer be possible to find.

## Taking part is voluntary

You can stop at any time by closing the page. You do not have to give a reason. You can skip any question you prefer not to answer, except the consent question.
