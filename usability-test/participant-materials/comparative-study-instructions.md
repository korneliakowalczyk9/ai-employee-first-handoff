# Trying out two versions of a prototype of an AI employee

Thank you for taking part. This takes about [40] minutes. You do it on your own, on a computer, in your own time. Please do it in one sitting.

## What this is

We are testing designs, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

The prototypes are mock-ups. They are not real AI employees and they do not send anything to anyone. All the people, companies and messages in them are made up.

You will use two versions of the same prototype, one after the other. They are called Version 1 and Version 2.

## The scenario

You are Maya. You run operations at a small company. Your company has set up an AI employee called Aria. This is the first time you are handing work to it. You would like Aria to help with this week's requests.

## Your task

In each version, work through this week's requests with Aria as you would in real life. Decide what you would like Aria to do, what you would prefer to do yourself, and when you are finished.

## The steps

1. Read the information and consent page, and choose whether you would like to record your screen. Recording is optional.
2. Answer a few background questions.
3. Open Version 1: [VERSION 1 LINK]
4. Use it as described below. Then go back to the questionnaire and answer the questions about Version 1.
5. Only after you have answered those questions, open Version 2: [VERSION 2 LINK]
6. Use it in the same way. Then go back to the questionnaire and answer the questions about Version 2. They are the same questions.
7. Answer the final questions about both versions.

Please complete Version 1 and its questions before you open Version 2. Please do not go back to Version 1 after you have opened Version 2.

## While you use each prototype

- Explore each one as you naturally would. Take as long as you need.
- Some screens may move on by themselves after a few seconds. That is expected.
- Please do not search for hints, ask someone for help or use other tools. We want to know how it works for you on your own.
- If something is unclear, carry on as you would normally. You can tell us about it afterwards.
- If something does not work or you get stuck, try once more. If it still does not work, carry on as best you can. If the page is blank, refresh it once. Then tell us what happened in the questionnaire. Please do not worry about it.
- When you feel you have finished, go back to the questionnaire. If you cannot continue because something is not working, return to the questionnaire and describe what happened.
- Please do not compare the versions in your answers until the final questions. Answer about the version you have just used.

## Optional screen recording

You can choose to record your screen while you use the prototypes. It is entirely optional, and you can take part in exactly the same way without it.

If you choose to record, you can record both versions in one recording or in two. Start recording before you open each prototype and stop when you go back to the questionnaire. Record only the browser window with the prototype. Please do not record your microphone or camera. Name each recording with your participant code and Version 1 or Version 2. If you make one recording of both, use "Version 1 and 2". Please do not use your name in the file name. The information page explains what the recording is for and how to send it.

## The questions afterwards

The questions ask what you did and what you understood. Please answer as you honestly remember it. You may choose "Not sure" where it is offered.

If you have a problem with a link or the questionnaire, contact [SUPPORT CONTACT]. We can help with technical problems only. We cannot answer questions about how the prototypes should work.
